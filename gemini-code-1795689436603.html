The 3 Possible Stories of Two Lines

Compare the coefficients (a, b, c) to predict how the lines behave without even drawing them.

Consistent • 1 Solution Case 1

1. Intersecting Lines

Analogy: Two Crossroads Meeting

The two lines have different slopes, so they cross each other at exactly one point. Because at least one solution exists, the pair is Consistent.

a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂ Unique Solution (1 crossing point)
Inconsistent • 0 Solutions Case 2

2. Parallel Lines

Analogy: Straight Train Tracks

Both lines tilt at the exact same angle, staying the same distance apart forever. They never meet, so there is no solution (Inconsistent).

a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ No Solution (Never touch)
Consistent • ∞ Solutions Case 3

3. Coincident Lines

Analogy: Two Cars on the Same Road

One equation is just a scaled-up copy of the other. Every point on Line 1 is also on Line 2—giving infinitely many solutions (Consistent & Dependent).

a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂ Infinitely Many Solutions
